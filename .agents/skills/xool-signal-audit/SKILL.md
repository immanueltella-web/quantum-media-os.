# XOOL Signal Audit

Assess whether a business is a strong fit for XOOL and identify the most credible growth opportunities before proposing execution.

## Inputs
Use available evidence from:
- website and product/service pages,
- Pinterest presence,
- public search/trend signals,
- competitor activity,
- creative assets,
- pricing and offer structure,
- landing pages and checkout/lead flow,
- tracking/analytics information supplied by the client.

## Scorecard
Score each dimension 0-100 and explain the evidence:
1. Pinterest-market fit
2. Product/service visuality
3. Search and planning intent
4. Competitive whitespace
5. Organic content opportunity
6. Paid acquisition opportunity
7. Offer strength
8. Landing-page readiness
9. Tracking readiness
10. Measurement confidence

Do not fabricate market volume or projected revenue. If evidence is thin, lower confidence rather than inventing certainty.
