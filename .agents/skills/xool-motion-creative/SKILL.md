# XOOL Motion Creative

Design short motion assets that communicate a product, benefit, transformation, process, or planning idea quickly.

## Best use cases
- product reveal or rotation,
- before/after or transformation,
- step-by-step sequence,
- hands-on product use,
- subtle cinematic loop,
- text-led explainer,
- seasonal/occasion inspiration,
- UGC-style concept with a clear hook.

## Motion brief
Define:
```text
Objective:
Audience intent:
Hook in first 1-2 seconds:
Length:
Aspect ratio:
Scene/shot sequence:
On-screen text:
Product/source assets:
Audio/voiceover need:
CTA:
Destination:
Test hypothesis:
```

## Production rules
- Keep the visual story coherent; do not make a slideshow of unrelated frames.
- Preserve real product and brand details when source assets are used.
- Use movement to clarify value, not merely decorate the post.
- Maintain text-safe areas and mobile legibility.
- Avoid fabricated results or unrealistic transformations.

## Output
Save motion briefs, frame plans, prompt logs, edit notes, and final asset references under `outputs/motion/`.
