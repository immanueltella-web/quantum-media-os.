# XOOL Pinterest Ads

Build paid media around controlled testing and measurable commercial outcomes.

## Read first
Use brand, offer, audience, Pinterest strategy, keyword map, creative outputs, CRO findings, and historical performance when available.

## Campaign planning
Define:
- commercial objective,
- conversion event,
- audience/keyword hypothesis,
- product/offer priority,
- funnel stage,
- creative hypothesis,
- landing page,
- budget envelope supplied or approved by the client,
- measurement window,
- success/failure thresholds.

## Test matrix
For each test record:
```text
Test ID:
Hypothesis:
Variable:

```

## Optimisation principles
- Change one meaningful variable at a time where practical.
- Diagnose creative, audience, offer, landing page, and tracking separately.
- Do not scale from CTR alone.
- Use revenue, CPA, conversion rate, AOV, margin context, and ROAS where available.
- Treat attribution windows and low-volume data carefully.

## Authority rule
Do not make live budget changes, launch campaigns, or submit ads without the applicable execution authority or explicit user instruction. Recommendations and draft structures may be prepared without repeated approval.

Save campaign plans, test matrices, audit notes, and optimisation recommendations under `outputs/ads/`.
