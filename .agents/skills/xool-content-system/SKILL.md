# XOOL Content System

Plan content as a commercial system, not a random posting calendar.

## Inputs
Read:
- `context/brand.md`
- `context/offer.md`
- `context/audience.md`
- `context/pinterest-strategy.md`
- `context/keyword-map.md`
- `context/best-performers.md`
- campaign/launch/event information

## Planning principles
Balance:
- discovery and inspiration,
- education and utility,
- product/service consideration,
- proof and trust,
- comparison and decision support,
- seasonal/occasion intent,
- direct conversion.

Each planned asset must have a measurable job.

## Output
Save to `outputs/calendars/content-calendar.md`:

```text
# XOOL Content Calendar
Period:
Primary commercial objective:
Primary KPI:

| Date | Platform | Funnel stage | Keyword/intent | Pillar | Format | Hook | Topic | Objective | Visual direction | Destination | CTA | Test hypothesis | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
```

## Pinterest-first rule
Pinterest content should be developed natively first. High-value ideas can then be adapted for Instagram, LinkedIn, Threads, X, TikTok, or other approved channels through `xool-social-repurposing`.

## Performance feedback
When `context/best-performers.md` exists:
- expand repeatable patterns,
- refresh rather than duplicate winners,
- reduce consistently weak formats,
- record what is being tested next and why.
