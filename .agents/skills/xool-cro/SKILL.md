# XOOL Conversion / CRO

Improve the path from Pin click to sale, lead, booking, or other agreed conversion.

## Audit dimensions
Review:
1. Message match between Pin/ad and destination
2. Above-the-fold clarity
3. Offer strength and specificity
4. Product/service proof
5. Trust and risk reduction
6. CTA prominence and friction
7. Mobile usability and speed signals
8. Navigation distractions
9. Checkout/form friction
10. Measurement and event tracking

## Evidence discipline
Separate:
- observed page facts,
- likely friction points,
- test hypotheses.

Do not promise conversion lifts before testing.
