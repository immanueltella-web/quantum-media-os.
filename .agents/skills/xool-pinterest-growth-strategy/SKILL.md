# XOOL Pinterest Growth Strategy

Turn brand and market evidence into a channel plan designed around commercial intent.

## Read first
Use, when present:
- `context/brand.md`
- `context/offer.md`
- `context/audience.md`
- `context/compliance.md`
- `outputs/audits/xool-signal-audit.md`
- `context/best-performers.md`

## Strategy questions
Answer:
1. What does the audience plan, search, save, compare, or buy on Pinterest?
2. Which planning moments create the strongest commercial opportunity?
3. Where can the brand enter before the buyer has chosen a supplier or product?
4. Which products/offers deserve organic, paid, shopping, or retargeting support?
5. Which creative angles are native to Pinterest rather than recycled social ads?
6. What must be measured to prove commercial value?
