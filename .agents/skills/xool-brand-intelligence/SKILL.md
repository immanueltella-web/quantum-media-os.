# XOOL Brand Intelligence

Create the brand foundation that every downstream XOOL skill must follow.

## Gather and verify
Collect, when available:
- brand name, website, handles, market and geography,
- products/services and price points,
- target audiences and buying triggers,
- business goals and commercial priorities,
- competitors and substitutes,
- voice, visual identity, colours, typography and logo rules,
- product imagery and approved assets,
- compliance or claim restrictions,
- proof assets such as reviews, case studies and verified results.

Use public research when available. Do not infer sensitive client facts or invent missing brand claims.

## Output files
Create or update:
- `context/brand.md`
- `context/offer.md`
- `context/audience.md`
- `context/compliance.md`
- `context/asset-library.md`
